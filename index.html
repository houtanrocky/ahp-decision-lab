THE METHOD

From your comparisons to a ranked list

01

Compare pairs

You express a relative preference on Saaty's 1–9 scale. Reverse comparisons are filled in as reciprocals.

A[i,j] = a; A[j,i] = 1/a
02

Calculate priorities

The selected solver turns each comparison matrix into weights that sum to one.

Eigenvector or row geometric mean
03

Review consistency

The consistency ratio flags comparisons that may conflict. It measures coherence, not correctness.

CR = CI / Random Index
04

Combine the results

Each option's local priority is multiplied by each criterion's weight and summed.

score = Σ(weight × local priority)
AHP organizes your judgments; it does not discover objective truth. Review the criteria, comparisons, and evidence behind your choice.

Privacy and your files
